/ 04 · ENVIRONMENT

Bring your secrets.
Leave nothing invented.

Copy the template, fill the blanks. Never commit a filled .env — validate with pnpm run env:check.

TELEGRAM_BOT_TOKEN REQUIRED
Bot identity for the chat channel. Register with @BotFather and paste.
SOLANA_RPC_URL
RPC endpoint for live chain ops. Helius RPC URL works out of the box.
WALLET_PRIVATE_KEY
Backend signer for real swaps and wallet sends. Keep it in .env only.
MOONSHOT_API_KEY PREFERRED
Kimi/Moonshot for agent intelligence. Free ClawdRouter takes over when unset.
HELIUS_API_KEY · BIRDEYE
Portfolio cascade data — any of Helius, Birdeye, or Solana Tracker keys unlock the market path.
VIBEBOT_EXECUTION_ADAPTER_TOKEN
Bearer token guarding the web execution adapter endpoint. Optional, but good hygiene.
